Name: Scareware
Description: Scareware is a type of social engineering that uses false alarms and threats to frighten users into believing their computer is infected or has a problem, prompting them to purchase or download fraudulent software.
Author: 'Claude'
Created: 2026-06-02
Techniques:
  - Technique: Scareware 
    Description: Scareware is a form of malicious software or social engineering engineering tactic designed to trick victims into believing their computer or device is severely infected, compromised, or facing legal trouble. By inducing panic, the attacker drives the user to buy useless software, download actual malware, or surrender sensitive information.
    Usecase: Tricking users into downloading malware
    Category: Technical Coercion
    MitreID: T1566.002
    Tags:
      - Coercion: Scareware

Safeguards: 
  - Control: Awareness
    Description: Educate employees on the common signs of scareware and to avoid clicking suspicious links. Emphasize the importance of not calling any phone numbers on the screen and to contact your IT team through established channels.
  - Control: Popup Blockers
    Description: Install a popup blocker on browsers.
    Link: https://www.malwarebytes.com/cybersecurity/basics/scareware
  - Control: Close Browser Window
    Description: Use Task Manager (Ctrl + Alt + Delete) or Force Quit (for Mac) to exit the browser or suspicious app. Use built in keyboard shortcuts to exit the fullscreen page.
    Link: https://www.malwarebytes.com/cybersecurity/windows/how-to-exit-full-screen-on-windows
